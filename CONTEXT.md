# Study RPG

A game built around practicing the project's medical question bank.

## Language

**Character progression**:
Persistent growth of the player's character through question practice, carrying forward between battles.
_Avoid_: Mastery (character growth does not by itself establish medical knowledge).

**Overall progression**:
Character growth shared across practice in all specialties, providing the character's basic combat strength.

**Specialty progression**:
Growth associated with practice in a particular specialty, unlocking distinctive abilities for that specialty.

**Question opportunity**:
A moment within a battle when answering a study question powers a combat action. Taking the opportunity is optional in ordinary battles.

**Boss question phase**:
A required moment in a boss battle during which the player attempts a study question. A correct answer is not required to proceed.

**Specialty adventure**:
An adventure whose question category matches the player's chosen specialty for practice.

**Mixed adventure**:
An adventure containing questions from a mix of the player's selected study categories.

**Question encounter**:
A study encounter along an adventure's route, distinct from a question opportunity within combat.

**Route map**:
The adventure view in which the player selects encounters along a route, rather than moving a character freely through a world.

**Recovery question**:
One different question from the same specialty offered after an incorrect answer in a question encounter. The route continues after this second attempt regardless of correctness; a missed original question remains in the redemption pool even if the recovery answer is correct.

**Redemption Adventure**:
An adventure accessed from a separate Redemption section, drawn from eligible previously incorrect questions. It is not a branch or node on the regular adventure route map.

**Redemption**:
Answering a previously incorrect question correctly in a subsequent adventure after the adventure in which it was missed is finished, earning the normal correct-answer reward and removing it from the redemption pool. Redemption does not establish medical mastery.

**Specialty completion**:
The state in which a specialty has no unanswered questions or questions awaiting redemption.

**Coins**:
Game currency earned from qualifying correct answers and battle victories, spendable on cosmetics and limited tactical items.

**Victory bonus**:
An additional coin reward built through consecutive battle victories and reset by defeat. It applies only to battles still eligible for their first-victory coin reward.

**Consumable recovery**:
Optional restoration of supplies spent in a failed battle, in exchange for a small coin fee or one new question attempt from the selected study scope. The question need not be answered correctly to restore those supplies.
