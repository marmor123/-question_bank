The lantern keeper’s note

Fictional reading fixture — not medical study material

You reach Mossgate at dusk. The keeper has left three lanterns beside a sealed archway: one blue, one amber, and one green. A note describes how the gate’s protective ward responds to them.

“The blue lantern lights the path but cannot open the gate. The amber lantern opens it only after the bell has rung. The green lantern opens it whenever the stream is quiet. If the bell rings while the stream is loud, every lantern remains locked until morning.”

The stream is quiet tonight. The bell’s rope is broken, and nobody has rung it. You may light only one lantern before the guardian returns.

Which lantern can open the gate under these conditions?
